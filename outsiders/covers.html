The Covers

Many sixties-influenced bands cite the Outsiders as one of their favourite groups, and many have covered Outsiders' songs on their records. You can also find plenty of Outsiders' covers on "Misfit - A Tribute To The Outsiders", a VV.AA. homage to the Outsiders published by Screaming Apple Records in 1994. This is a listing of covers I'm aware of, you are welcome to contribute with additional entries.
