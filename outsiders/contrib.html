Steve runs the The Fleshtones Hall of Fame, an excellent web page about this fantastic band. If you like the 'tones you absolutely HAVE to visit.
SANDER VAN MALSSEN (svm@kozmix.ow.nl)

ALAN WRIGHT (dothepop@ix.netcom.com)
Alan and his wife Lisa do a mag called Do the pop! about '70s punk, powerpop and garage style music which you really should try out.
Alan also was (from 89 to 91) in the 14th Wray (one track on a 7" EP on Moxie Records and one on the compilation LP "Fun With Mushrooms" on Delirium) who used to play the Outsiders' "Sun's Going Down" in their live repertoire.
