Covers and Tributes



[1991 - Merge / City Slang - LC6853 - 7"]

Seam
"Look Back In Anger"

Side B of the Granny 9X single. A punky and noisy version of TVPs' classic.

Sixtynine and the Continuous People
"Scream Quietly / The Painted Word / La Grande Illusion / King And Country / A Long Time Gone / Brian's Magic Car"

A TVPs-obsessed band from Italy. First Ever Gig is, as the title suggest, the recording of their first gig. It also includes a cover of the Creation's "Painter Man".
[1998 - Mind's Eye - ME-DEMO 001 - CD Demo]

Sixtynine and the Continuous People
"David Hockney's Diaries / The Girl Who Had Everything / Painted Word (Reprise)"

Again a live recording, Reprise includes very long and psychedelic versions of "David Hockney" and "Painted Word". Also on this CD a cover of 14 Iced Bears' "Take It".
[1999 - Mind's Eye - ME-DEMO 002 - CD Demo]

Sixtynine and the Continuous People
"I Know Where Syd Barrett Lives"

An excellent international tribute to Syd Barrett, On A Distant Shore includes this very atmospheric rendition of Television Personalities' classic.... a tribute within a tribute?.
[2001 - Windless Air - CD]


[1994 - Direct  / Little Teddy - LITE 726 - 7"]

Colin Swan
"Silly Girl / I Know Where Syd Barrett Lives / Bridget Riley / A Picture Of Dorian Gray"

A Portrait Of Dan Treacy is a loving tribute by Colin Swan of the Direct Hits (whose first LP was published by Dan Treacy's Whaam! records). Originally released via Direct Tapes in the mid eighties.


The Tables
"In A Perfumed Garden"

The Tables are often dubbed as the Norwegian TVPs, which they aren't, since they have a very personal and own style which bears only very superficial resemblances to TVPs'. Their brilliant debut Shady Whims & Obstacles includes a great instro version of "In A Perfumed Garden" made only with an ocarina and percussions which sound like, and probably are, kitchen pans!
[1990 - Shtooopid - OOO 01 - LP]
[1992 - Perfect Pop - POP 7 - CD]
