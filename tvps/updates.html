Updates History


28 May 2004

News
- Alistair Fitchett still likes vinyl 7” singles. "Most of my favourite labels seemed to be built around 7” singles, and all did something special with the format". So he started Unpopular, a label of 7” vinyl singles. UNPOP 2 is Lovejoy's: "Strike A Pose", which features a cover of TVPs' "Someone To Share My Life With" on the flipside. Also announced: UNPOP 5: "The Legend sings the songs of Daniel Treacy"

28 May 2004

News
- Tòm and Jane love vinyl 7” singles too. They thought "there should be more of them around" and so they started the Félicité Singles Club. CITÉ 06 is Sixtynine And The Continuous People's "I Can't Hear You (Anymore)" which features on side AA "Shadows That Haunt Me", an unreleased Television Personalities' song, the only known version being from a 1988 Dan Treacy solo acoustic live gig. Amongst the quotations scattered on the sleeve artwork there are many verses from TVPs' "Haunted"

23 May 2004

News
- Out now on Discos Harte: "Harte Para Todos!": a spanish tribute EP to the Television Personalities ... in spanish! The bands and tracks featured are: TCR: "Yo recuerdo a Bridget Riley (I remember Bridget Riley)", Alpino: "Tiempo hostil (Smashing time)", Thy Surfin' Eyes: "Diario de un hombre joven (Diary of a young man)" and Los Part-Time Pops: "Quiero disfrutar del hilo musical (Favourite films)." Some lyrics are faithful to the originals, while some are... not so much. The EP's liner notes feature an interesting story about (and homage to) Dan Treacy.

News
- The April issue (#3 / 2004) of Swedish music magazine Groove came with a free-to-subscribers-only compilation CD which includes TVP's "The Engine Driver" Song covered by The Higher Elevations.

9 February 2004

News
- Swedish Pop Sensation Jens Lekman debuted with this CDEP in October 2003 after one year of limited edition CDRs (ranging from 1 to 250 copies) and hit #11 in the Swedish Charts. The EP features features a cover of TVPs' "Someone To Share My Life With", while a sample of the vocalise that opens the TVPs original was used in the EP's hit title track "Maple Leaves". The record was re-released in Feb 2004 by US label Secretly Canadian.

News
- US pop punk band The Mr. T Experience pay a loving homage to Dan Treacy on their new CD EP "The Miracle Of Shame" with an acoustic song called "I Don't Know Where Dan Treacy Lives". The song refers to a period when Dan Treacy "disappeared" and no one was really sure where he was. The reference point is obviously the classic TVPs masterpiece "I Know Where Syd Barrett Lives" and the lyrics are equally sweet and cruel, not totally unlike Dan Treacy's own style.

News
- New on Cherry Red Records: Part Time Punks: The Very Best of Television Personalities. Here's Cherry Red's official release note: "Emerging from the punk/indie explosion of the late 70's and a career spanning more than 15 years Television Personalities have become the band to define indie pop. This 22 track album was compiled by Dan Treacy, the artistic genius behind the name. The sleeve notes are written by Alan McGee of Creation Records where he describes the TVP's as his favourite band ever and acknowledges the influence they had on him setting up Creation Records."
