All songs written by Dan Treacy and published by Twist And Shout Music. Remastered by Phil Vinall and Barry Woodward at Townhouse. Sleve recreation by Hand And Eye.

"The Painted Word", alongside Big Star's "Sisters Lovers" and any one of Nick Drake's LPs is one of the best emotionally introspective LPs I've ever heard. Dan Treacy's songs tear me apart. This is their finest moment. Both the humor and tragedy of the songs are beautiful. It deserves re-release. Well done Fire.
[Alan McGee (Creation Records) June 1990]

Television Personalities The Painted Word was recorded in 1982/83 at The Hit Factory. The artistes were Daniel Treacy, Joseph Foster, David Musker and Mark Flunder, with Ingrid Weiss, David Walmsley and Martin Kerr-Daley. Produced by J. Foster & D. Treacy. Original sleeve design by Ingrid & Joe. Original cover photo by Alison Turner.
