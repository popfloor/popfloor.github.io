Note: The original LP release of Chocolat Art didn't include the medley. A special limited ed. tape with the full show, called I Know Where Dan Treacy Lives, was released. Later the CD version of Chocolat Art included the full show as well. The liner notes below are from the CD version. The LP version had no sleeve notes.
