1981 - Whaam! - WHAAM 001 - 7"

Songs:
Painting By Numbers

Notes:
"The Gifted Children" was the new name that Dan chose when Ed Ball left the band, but he used it only on this single and on the "All For Art And Art For All" VV.AA. compilation. Click here to read the story of the Gifted Children.
Both songs were included in TVPs' second album ("Mummy Your Not Watching Me"). In the album "Lichtenstein Girl" was retitled "Lichtenstein Painting".
