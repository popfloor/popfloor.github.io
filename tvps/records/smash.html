"I'd prefer to remain a mystery; I never like to give my backgrounds and, anyway, I make it all different all the time I'm asked. It's not just that it's part of my "image" not to tell, it's just that I forget what I said the day before and I have to make it all up again. I don't think I have an "image" anyway, favourable or unfavourable." [Geoffrey Ingrams].
But the "image" of Geoffrey Ingrams is in fact a mystery. HE is a cult hero, acclaimed for his prints, for his paintings, and for his films, though it is for his work in the cinema that he is best known, topically, his latest film, "MARLEY and the GIRLBOMBS", following the success of "Helicopters", and his earlier "non-visual" pictures. His desire to involve his audience in a total experience was what originally led him to open his own club, THE WOW? CLINIC in London. The thought provoking, disturbing and sensational images seen and felt there required an appropriate aural dimension and it was to achieve a degree of control over the music that he decided to form his own group, TV PERSONALITIES. The many other demands on his time and talents meant he could not for long remain an active member of the group, but his instigation of it had predestined its future, and he left an indelible mark on its style. After his departure the group consisted of: Craig Sinclair (guitar, vocal, sitar), J.J. Myers (bass guitar, vocal, autoharp), Phil Tomaire (harmonium, dulcimer) Matthew Spencer Robertson (percussion). [Bradley Emmit Jnr.]
