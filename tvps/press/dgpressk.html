The Television Personalities released their first single ‘Part-Time Punks’ in 1979 and continued to release records throughout the 1980s and 90s on various labels. Their single ‘Salvador Dali’s Garden Party’ was a minor hit and their cult status remained throughout this time.
Three years on from its recording at the esteemed Toe Rag studios, London and the studios of WYNU, New York this great album from the Television Personalities will finally be released this autumn.
It is their last ever recording and features versions of 10 of Dan Tracey’s favourite songs. These include Psychic TV’s ‘Godstar’, Jonathan Richman’s ‘Pablo Picaso’ and Razorcuts ‘Sorry To Embarrass You’ (which recently featured on the Cheap Damaged Goods Sampler CD).
