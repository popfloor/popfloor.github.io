Dan Treacy Benefit

New York-based group The Baskervilles are organising a benefit show
with all proceeds to be used to fund new recording sessions for Dan Treacy.

The event is scheduled for Friday, July 30th at Sin-e' in New York.

The following bands will play:
The Mad Scene
My Favorite
Baskervilles
The Insomniacs
Burnside Project
notTVPs
