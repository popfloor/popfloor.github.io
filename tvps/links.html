Television Personalities Sites (Updated 12/03/2001)

  • A Day In Heaven: Keg's TVPs Website
    Keg is the man I love to hate. On his site you will find some amazing features like "Unreleased" (a well documented article on TVPs unreleased records & songs), "Set Lists" (a comprehensive database of TVPs gigs with set lists) and "Treacyworld" (a chronological exploration of Dan Treacy's songs). All things I've been wanting to do for ages but never found the time.... as a TVPs website creator my guts are wrenched with envy, but as a fan I'm truly grateful for such an excellent job!
  • How I learned to love the ... Television Personalities: Marco's TVPs Website
    Are you interested in trading TVPs live tapes? Here you can find Marco's list. Also on the site: a small but interesting picture gallery and other features.
  • Television Personalities: A Japanese website
  • TV Personalities Guitar Tabs: A growing collection of TVPs guitar tabs, some even with "play along" Flash movies!
  • TVPersonalities Mailing List: The eGroups website for the TVPersonalities Mailing List: a great chance to contact other TVPs fans and share opinions and news. You can read the messages posted so far and subscribe to the list.

Labels (Updated 31/03/2002)
The following labels released TVPs' records and run their own mailorder.

Bouncing Corp: run by Hans-Martin Gross, formerly of Pastell records, which released "Chocolate Art".
Cherry Red have a wide & very diverse catalogue.
Check Damaged Goods' web site for their cool garage & pop catalogue.
Fire is home of the first four TVPs albums reissues and of all early 90's TVPs output.
Little Teddy have a small but very cool mailorder catalogue with releases from great labels such as Overground, Perfect Pop, Damaged Goods, Vinyl Japan, Bi-Joopiter, K, Detour and bands such as The Bartlebees, McTells, Silly Pillows and many others. Lots of non-Litlle Teddy TVPs releases too!.
After a period of inactivity Overground is back.
Perfect Pop live up to their name. Click and order!
Check Vinyl Japan's web site for their cool garage & pop catalogue.
